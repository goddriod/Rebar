Saves all projects and prices. Restoring adds the file’s projects as new projects; nothing is overwritten. Works when the app is opened from your own web link.

    Cutting plan

    Every cut length of this tab’s members is packed into stock bars per diameter (kerf included). Black ticks = saw cuts; hatched = remnant, green = usable off-cut. Weight = d²/162.2 kg/m.

    Project summary: stock bars

    Enter the price of one stock bar for each diameter to see costs. Total bars = sum of each tab’s own cutting plan (e.g. 12 in Column + 7 in Beam = 19). “If packed together” = count if off-cuts were shared across tabs. ⚠ = a piece is longer than the stock bar.
